Ultimate User Guide

Everything you need to know to master the Billing APP, from initial setup to maintaining your entire shop's ledger.

1

Initial Setup & Printer Configuration

Before creating your first bill, it is highly recommended to configure your shop details and printer.

  • Shop Profile: Go to Settings and enter your Shop Name & Phone Number. These will appear on all your printed bills and PDFs.
  • Printer Setup: The Billing APP strictly supports 80mm (3-inch) Thermal Printers to ensure standard professional receipt formatting.
  • You can connect your printer via USB, Bluetooth, or Network (Wi-Fi/LAN). Go to the Printer Configuration tab and pair your 80mm thermal printer seamlessly.
  • Language: You can enable Regional Language support while creating bills if you want to print item names in either language as per your requirement.
2

Inventory & Stock Management

Organize your products effectively to speed up billing later.

  • Add Categories: Open the Inventory tab and create categories (e.g., Groceries, Electronics). You can assign a specific color to each category for quick visual identification.
  • Add Items: Inside a category, add new items. Provide a standard English Name, a Regional Name, and set the default Rate along with the master unit.
  • Units Setup: Define a Master Unit (e.g., Kg) and an Alternate Unit (e.g., GRAM). The app will automatically handle the math while billing.
  • Maintain Stock: Use the Stock tab to record incoming stock. The app automatically deducts stock when you generate a bill. Low stock alerts will help you re-order in time.
3

Creating Bills using Smart Num Pad

Generating invoices is the fastest part of the app thanks to our optimized layouts.

  • Choose a Layout: You can select between List, Grid, or Hybrid layout. We highly recommend the Hybrid Layout for the best experience.
  • Smart Num Pad: Do not waste time typing names. Use the smart on-screen Num Pad to quickly tap quantities and enter amounts. It fetches the default rate automatically.
  • Edit on the Fly: If you are giving a special discount, you can manually edit the rate of an item specifically for that single bill without changing the master inventory rate.
  • Checkout: Review the cart, select a saved Customer/Party (or enter a new name), and click the Print icon to instantly push the receipt to your 80mm thermal printer.
4

Managing Parties & Ledger (Khata)

Keep track of your regular customers and their outstanding balances effortlessly.

  • Add a Party: In the Parties/Ledger tab, you can add your regular customers with their phone numbers and addresses.
  • Give Credit: When creating a bill for a saved party, you can mark the bill as "Unpaid" or "Partial Payment". The pending amount is automatically added to their Ledger balance.
  • Receive Payments: When a customer pays off their debt, go to their Ledger profile and record a "Payment Received" entry. This will immediately reduce their outstanding balance.
  • Full History: You can view a chronological list of every purchase they made and every payment they gave, ensuring zero disputes over money.
5

Bill History & Sharing PDF

Never lose track of a past transaction.

  • Bill History: Navigate to the History tab to see every invoice ever generated, sorted by date.
  • Search: Use the search bar to find a specific bill by the Customer's Name or the exact Date of billing.
  • Re-print or Share: Open any old bill to re-print it to your thermal printer. If the customer is not in the shop, click the Share PDF button. The app will generate a highly professional A4/Receipt PDF that you can send directly via WhatsApp or Email.
6

Cloud Sync & Data Backup

Your shop's data is precious. Keep it safe.

  • Google Login: Sign in with your Google account from the app settings.
  • Automatic Backup: Once signed in, you can easily backup your entire inventory, ledger, and bill history to the secure cloud servers.
  • Multi-Device Sync: If you upgrade your phone or want to run the app on a Windows PC simultaneously, simply log in with the exact same Google account and restore your data in one tap.
